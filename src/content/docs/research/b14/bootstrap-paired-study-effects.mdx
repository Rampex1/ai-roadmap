Calculate a <Term id="bootstrap-paired-study-effects" /> within each matched unit, then summarise the distribution across units. If the question concerns training randomness, the independent unit is usually a training seed or a larger independently sampled split. Hundreds of test examples from one trained model do not supply hundreds of independent training runs.

A paired bootstrap draws complete paired effects with replacement and recomputes their mean. Do not sample baseline and candidate independently; that loses matching. Show every seed result when there are few pairs. A percentile interval with very few pairs can look deceptively precise if the observed effects are similar.

This exercise returns a deterministic list of bootstrap means given a fixed seed. It assumes complete already-paired finite scalar effects and does not promote the interval to proof of causal or external validity.

## Worked example

All matched effects are the same, so every bootstrap mean is two.

```python
def bootstrap_effects(effects,draws,seed):
    from random import Random
    if not effects or draws<=0:
        raise ValueError("need effects and positive draws")
    rng=Random(seed)
    n=len(effects)
    return [sum(effects[rng.randrange(n)] for _ in range(n))/n for _ in range(draws)]

print(bootstrap_effects([2,2],3,4))
```

```text title="Output"
[2.0, 2.0, 2.0]
```
